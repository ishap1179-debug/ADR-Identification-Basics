# ADR-Identification-Basics
ADR Identification Basics – Pharmacovigilance Task 1

### Patient Scenario
- Patient: Female, 45 years
- Symptoms: Rash, itching, nausea and dizziness
- Suspected medicine: Amoxicillin

### Analysis
The patient developed rash, itching, nausea and dizziness after starting amoxicillin. The symptoms are temporally associated with the medication, suggesting a possible adverse drug reaction.

### Suspected Drug
Amoxicillin

### Possible ADR
Allergic/hypersensitivity reaction to amoxicillin.

### Conclusion
Based on the patient's symptoms and the timeline of amoxicillin use, amoxicillin is the suspected drug responsible for the adverse drug reaction.
